Hint Effects (TRUE / LIE / FAKE)

A player may place one hint per turn on their own board. You guess TRUE, LIE, or FAKE. Effects depend on the real hint and your guess.

When You Guess

Real hint Your guess Effect
TRUE LIE / FAKE About half of revivable destroyed cells on the opponent board revive (misses and hits on ships that are not fully sunk), rounded.
TRUE TRUE No effect.
LIE TRUE / FAKE Opponent gains 2 bonus shots on their next turn.
LIE LIE A forced correct hint is added about the opponent.
FAKE TRUE / LIE Opponent places a TRUE hint on your field at the start of opponent's next turn.
FAKE FAKE You place a TRUE hint on the opponent field (hint type is restricted to opponent consumed hint types).

Introduction & Game Flow

Battleship Bluff is a pass-and-play game for two players. You place fleets, attack hidden targets, and add bluff hints that can change the next turns.

  1. Setup: each player places ships (length 5, 4, 3, 3, 2), then locks in and passes.
  2. Turn start: current player receives base shots (plus any bonus).
  3. Optional hint: before the first shot, reveal your own board and place one hint card.
  4. Attack: choose a target cell on the opponent board.
  5. If an incoming hint is active, you guess TRUE or LIE, confirm, then effects are applied.
  6. Shot resolves as hit or miss. Hits can grant extra shots if enabled.
  7. When shots end, pass device to the next player.
  8. Game ends when one side sinks all enemy ships, or if someone ends the game.
